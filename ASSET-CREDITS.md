# MYLIFTCOACH 2.0 photographic asset register

The app now loads photographic JPG assets directly from this repository; the original vector marble and equipment drawings were removed. The photographs are source files, not UI screenshots or decorative CSS cover layers.

- `assets/emerald-marble-photo.jpg`: teal/gold fluid-marble photographic artwork by **FlyD**. Original work: https://unsplash.com/photos/blue-and-white-abstract-painting-vf68N-uaifE . The saved JPG was color-graded to a medium emerald/teal tone (the change is encoded into the image file).
- `assets/hero-dumbbells-photo.jpg`: real metal dumbbells in a dark gym by **Jason Grant**. Original: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-row-of-dumbs-in-a-gym-m4Jqyv5VwqY .

Both were published as free-to-use under the Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license (accessed November 2026). Attribution is appreciated but not required. A local workflow `.github/workflows/import-photo-assets.yml` downloads and processes the source image files.

**Design target:** use the approved emerald-marble, champagne-gold MYLIFTCOACH 2.0 eight-screen mockup as the reference. These are real photographic assets and a significant improvement over the vectors, but they are **not** pixel-identical source images from the concept artwork. The rendering should be reviewed on iPhone before calling the visual redesign approved.

**Safety:** Original `gcfd8hy5mb-ai/workout-tracker` and existing Supabase workout records have not been changed by this image integration.
